What this MVP covers

Scroll Sense
Tracks scrolling time against your limit (20 min by default, like an Instagram-style timer) and how negative the content is. Nothing leaves your browser.
Gentle Pause
A short, friendly check-in when the score crosses your threshold.
Calm Swap
A guided breath, a walk or calm music instead of the next swipe.
Time Reclaimed
Insights tab: scroll minutes, nudge acceptance rate, mood trend.

Simulated for the pilot: the feed is fictional headlines, and each accepted swap counts as 10 minutes reclaimed (an assumption to validate with users). A real build would read usage signals through Android or iOS screen-time APIs.
